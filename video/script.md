## How to record it (about 60 minutes)

1. **Room.** Quiet, fan off, window closed. Phone or laptop at eye level, light in front of you, not
   behind.
2. **Face parts** (hook, why me, close): QuickTime → File → New Movie Recording. At most three takes
   each; keep the best.
3. **Screen parts:** Cmd + Shift + 5 → Options → Microphone: MacBook microphone → Record Entire
   Screen. Do the site demo while saying the lines.
4. **The "why voice" part** reuses your Emergent screen recording from Friday or Saturday. Pick a
   10–15 second clip of you speaking a prompt.
5. **Edit in iMovie:**
   - drag the clips in order: hook, demo, Emergent clip, evidence, why me, close;
   - trim the pauses;
   - **keep the total under 2:55**;
   - add a title with the URL at the end.
6. **Export:** File → Share → File → 1080p.

## How to upload it (15 minutes)

1. Go to studio.youtube.com → Create → Upload videos, and pick the file.
2. **Title:** Nikhil Balamurugan · the long version (Wispr Flow India challenge).
3. **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids".
4. **Visibility:** Unlisted. Save. Copy the link.
5. **Captions:** Subtitles → this video → the automatic English captions → Duplicate and edit. Fix
   Skipp, Drishti, Recoup, Wispr Flow and Emergent → Publish.
6. **Test:** open the link in an incognito window. It must play without signing in.
