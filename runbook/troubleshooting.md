# Part 9 · If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The Emergent credits haven't arrived after 10 hours | Check spam. Then email support@emergent.sh from your Flow email: "I registered for the Wispr Flow hiring challenge credits on [date] with this email and haven't received them." Tell me too. |
| Emergent has no Publish option | Stop and tell me before building more. The Free plan can't publish. We decide by Saturday noon: a paid Emergent plan, or another vibe-coding tool the challenge allows, used the same way by voice (and said so on the site). |
| Emergent rewrote your text | Say: "You changed my wording in the [section] section. Put back the exact text from the content I pasted, and don't edit my words again." |
| Emergent broke something | Say: "Undo your last change. Keep everything else exactly as it was." If a publish broke the live site: Manage Publishing → roll back. |
| Emergent keeps adding things you didn't ask for | Say: "Stop. Only build what I asked for. Remove the [thing]." |
| Something scrolls sideways on the phone | Say: "Something on the page is wider than a 375-pixel screen. Find it and fix only that." |
| The squeeze effect doesn't show | Say the scaleX line in Part 3, step 4. |
| Flow keeps mishearing a name | Add it to Flow's dictionary, say it again, and note it in your voice log. |
| Flow mangles colour codes, ids or links | Use the snippets (Part 3, last section), or paste. Note it in your voice log. |
| Out of Flow words for the week | Dictate on your phone, or use the student offer or Pro trial. Tell me. |
| The mic doesn't work on the site | Test the **published** URL, not Emergent's preview: previews can block the microphone. Firefox doesn't support it at all; that's expected. |
| The video runs over 3:00 | Cut the demo to one question, and drop the reviews line. |
| No time for a test | Skip it. The site says "not run". Never estimate a result. |
| The form won't take the date | Use the date picker, not typing. The date is 09/12/2026. |
